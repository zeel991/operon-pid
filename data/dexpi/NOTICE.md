# Data attribution

The files in `C08/` are unmodified copies from the DEXPI initiative's public test cases:
<https://gitlab.com/dexpi/TrainingTestCases>, folder `dexpi 1.3/example pids/C08 Two connected P&IDs (Covestro)/C08V01`.

They are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), the license of that repository.
The drawings and lists are example data contributed by Covestro to the DEXPI test cases. The "CONFIDENTIAL"
stamp is part of the published sample drawing.

Files used: `UER-1234567.pdf`, `UER-2345678.pdf`, `COV Equipment List.xlsm`, `COV Instrument List.xlsm`,
`COV Line List.xls`, `COV Valve List.xlsm`. The spreadsheets are read cell by cell with openpyxl/xlrd; their
macros are never run.
